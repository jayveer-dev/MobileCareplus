# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first preview

The first public version of Mobile Care Plus, for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) and macOS 27 on Apple
Silicon. Free and open source under GPL-3.0.

**Phones**
- Works with iPhone and Android, over a USB cable.
- Home: phone model, system version, battery, storage, restart and shut down.

**Manage**
- Photos & videos: browse by date, album or folder; view full size; copy to your computer;
  delete (Android: to the phone's Trash; iPhone: on a Mac).
- Apps: see installed apps with their icons, and uninstall them.
- iPhone contacts and messages: read and export.

**Backup & Restore**
- iPhone: full backups (optionally encrypted) and restore; save 9 kinds of data as normal files.
- Android: save photos, contacts and calendars to your computer, and restore them to a phone.

**Move to a new phone**
- Pick the old and new phone and see what can move in that direction.
- To an Android phone: photos & videos, contacts and calendars.

**Chat transfer**
- WhatsApp and WhatsApp Business: back up, read and export chats from iPhone.
- Android: find WhatsApp's chat backup, copy it to your computer, and read it with your 64-digit key
  (new; still being tested with real backups).

**Toolbox**
- Screenshot of the phone's screen (Android; iPhone only in Developer Mode).
